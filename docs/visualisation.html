Overview

What this subsystem does

The visualisation subsystem renders a live holographic twin of the robot workspace in the Quest 3 AR passthrough view. The UR3e arm, trolley, and detected cubes are displayed as 3D overlays anchored to the physical scene.

Joint states and cube poses flow from ROS via the ROS-TCP-Endpoint bridge. The Unity app subscribes to these topics and updates the rendered models in real time. The trolley scene is loaded at startup with the correct 180° orientation matching the physical layout.

Quest 3 renders virtual objects composited over the passthrough camera. Spatial anchors keep holograms stable as user moves.

Known Issues

Troubleshooting

  • Arm hologram doesn't move
    Check that /joint_states is being published. The UR driver must be active. With URSim, External Control must be playing in Polyscope.
  • Cubes not visible in AR
    Cubes only appear in the headset after first detection by the perception node. Point the Brio at a cube face until RViz shows the marker — it then publishes to Quest.
  • Holograms drift from physical objects
    Hand-eye calibration must be run and loaded. A stale or missing calibration causes the camera-to-base TF to be incorrect, offsetting all cube positions.
  • Quest can't connect
    Ensure Quest and laptop are on the same WiFi. The launcher prints the correct IP at startup. Enter it manually in ROS Settings if UDP auto-discovery doesn't work.
